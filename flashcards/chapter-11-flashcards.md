## Chapter 11: Full SC-300 Exam Simulation & Time-Management Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | SC-300 exam approx. question count and time limit? | Roughly 40-60 questions in about 120 minutes. Exam tip: budget ~2 min per standalone question, allow more per case-study block. |
| 2 | PIM 'Activation maximum duration' allowed range? | 1-24 hours, admin-configurable per role. Exam tip: 24 hours is the ceiling; a common default seen in scenarios is 8 hours. |
| 3 | Graph PowerShell cmdlet to view a user's risk state (used in case-study investigation questions)? | Get-MgRiskyUser -UserId <id> (Microsoft.Graph.Identity.SignIns module). Exam tip: configuration questions test exact cmdlet/module naming for governance and risk tasks. |
| 4 | PIM vs Entitlement Management — when to use which? | PIM = just-in-time elevation for privileged ADMIN ROLES (time-bound, approval, activation). Entitlement Management = self-service CATALOG for RESOURCE ACCESS (groups/apps/sites) with packages and expiration. Exam tip: 'privileged role' cue → PIM; 'catalog/self-service resource request' cue → Entitlement Management. |
| 5 | Conditional Access vs Identity Protection risk policies? | Conditional Access = policy engine enforcing controls (MFA, block, session) based on conditions including risk. Identity Protection = calculates the risk signal (user risk, sign-in risk) that Conditional Access consumes. Exam tip: modern risk-based policies are configured as Conditional Access policies referencing risk conditions. |
| 6 | Dynamic groups vs Access Reviews for time-bound access? | Dynamic groups: attribute-based auto membership, no built-in expiration. Access Reviews: add periodic recertification/expiration logic on top of any group or app assignment. Exam tip: 'auto-expire unless approved' cue = Access Reviews, not dynamic groups. |
| 7 | Time-budget rule for SC-300 case studies? | Case studies often consume ~20% of total time for ~15% of questions. Exam tip: skim exhibits ONCE, answer all questions for that case study before moving on — don't re-read the scenario per question. |
| 8 | Best approach to flagged interactive/drag-and-drop items? | Answer your best guess immediately, never leave blank, flag it, continue, and return only if time remains. Exam tip: an unanswered flagged item scores zero; a guessed one has a chance. |
| 9 | Elimination strategy for 'technically real but wrong fit' distractors? | Ask whether the option satisfies EVERY constraint in the scenario, not just the general topic. Exam tip: PIM, Entitlement Management, and Access Reviews are frequently paired as distractors for each other — match the specific verb (activate a role vs request a resource vs recertify). |
| 10 | Domain cue: 'approval workflow before elevating to Global Admin for 2 hours' | Points to Privileged Identity Management, Identity Governance domain. Exam tip: numeric time-bound + role elevation = PIM signal. |
| 11 | Domain cue: 'partner/guest requests access from a catalog and a manager approves' | Points to Entitlement Management access packages, Identity Governance domain. Exam tip: 'catalog' + 'external/guest' + 'approval' = Entitlement Management. |
| 12 | Final-minutes review strategy? | Prioritize in this order: fully unanswered items, then flagged-but-answered items, then double-checks. Exam tip: never submit a fully blank question — an educated guess beats zero credit. |
