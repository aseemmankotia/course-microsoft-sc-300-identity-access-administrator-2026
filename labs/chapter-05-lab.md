# Chapter 5 Lab: Microsoft Entra ID Protection: Risk Detections & Risk Policies

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Configure a Conditional Access policy using sign-in risk as a condition to require MFA at medium risk, and a separate user risk policy requiring a secure password change at high risk

## Key takeaway

User risk implies a compromised credential and demands a password change; sign-in risk implies a suspicious session and is satisfied by MFA.
